I am a **Master's student in Computational Science and Engineering** at **Harvard University** (Sep 2025 – May 2027, expected). I received my **B.Eng. in Electronic Information Engineering (AI Program)** from **Wuhan University** (GPA 3.86/4.0, Top 5%, National Scholarship, Top 1%).

I have been fortunate to work with [Prof. Haoran You](https://www.haoranyou.com/) on efficient LLM inference, [Prof. Yiyu Shi](https://engineering.nd.edu/faculty/yiyu-shi/) on fairness in medical speech and language interfaces, and [Prof. Lei Yu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=Klc_GHUAAAAJ) on event-based vision.

**Research Interests:** Efficient inference and serving for large language and vision-language models · Diffusion LLM decoding · LLM/VLM-as-a-judge and model evaluation · Knowledge distillation and model compression · Large-scale recommendation and advertising systems · Trustworthy and fair AI
