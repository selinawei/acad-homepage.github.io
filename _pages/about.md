I am a Master's student in **Computational Science and Engineering** at **Harvard University**, cross-registered at MIT EECS. I received my B.Eng. in Electronic Information Engineering (AI Program) from **Wuhan University**.

My focus is on developing efficient methods for training and inference of language models, across both autoregressive and diffusion LLMs. I have been fortunate to work with [Prof. Haoran You](https://www.haoranyou.com/), [Prof. Yiyu Shi](https://engineering.nd.edu/faculty/yiyu-shi/), and [Prof. Lei Yu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=Klc_GHUAAAAJ), and to intern at **Adobe** and **Xiaohongshu**.
